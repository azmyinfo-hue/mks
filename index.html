About MKS Group

Inspired by precision. Built to last.

We began our careers delivering residential, commercial, healthcare and industrial projects in Kuwait. We founded MKS Group in New Maadi, Cairo to bring the same engineering-led, documented way of working to Egypt, with a licensed design office, a site team and a quality team under one roof.

What we do

Complete solutions for every space.

Civil and structural

Excavation, concrete foundations, columns and slabs, retaining walls, masonry, waterproofing and facades.

Turnkey finishing

Interior design, gypsum ceilings, joinery, stone, flooring and decorative lighting for offices, venues and villas.

MEP and smart power

Distribution boards, power cabling, plumbing, firefighting and HVAC coordination, planned around the structure.

Landscape lighting

Garden, facade and boundary lighting with insulated underground cabling that turns outdoor space into architecture.

Engineering and drawings

3D and BIM coordination, clash detection, shop drawings, as-built records and value engineering.

Metal formwork

Aluminum and steel system formwork, climbing and slip forms and lean methods that shorten the floor cycle.

How we build

We build it on screen first.

Most delay and rework starts at the drawing stage, not on site, so that is where we put our discipline. Scroll to follow a package from tender to handover.

Review the tender

Constructability and BOQ review of the drawings and specifications, so scope is clear before work begins.

Coordinate in 3D

Structural, architectural and MEP models are overlaid and clashes are resolved on screen with documented reports.

Issue shop drawings

Coordinated drawings for formwork, reinforcement, openings, ceilings, joinery and service routes go to the consultant for approval.

Approve materials and mock-ups

Samples, method statements and full-scale mock-ups are signed off before bulk procurement.

Build under hold points

Concrete, cabling and finishes pass inspection before the next stage starts, with regular progress, quality and safety reports.

Test, hand over, support

Commissioning, snagging and as-built drawings, then O&M manuals, warranties and support during the defects period.
